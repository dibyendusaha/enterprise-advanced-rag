# Enterprise Procurement, Software Licensing & Vendor Onboarding Policy

## 1. Purchase Order (PO) Approval Thresholds
All third-party software, cloud subscriptions, and hardware purchases require an approved Purchase Requisition (PR) in NexaProcure before vendor engagement. Purchases up to INR 1,00,000 require Department Head approval; purchases between INR 1,00,001 and INR 10,00,000 require VP + Finance Controller approval and at least 3 competitive vendor quotes; purchases exceeding INR 10,00,000 require CFO sign-off.

## 2. Mandatory NDA & Master Services Agreement (MSA)
No proprietary specifications or datasets may be shared with a prospective vendor until a bilateral Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) is executed via DocuSign. Standard Legal review SLA is 5 business days for NexaCore paper and 10 business days for third-party vendor paper.

## 3. Vendor Payment Terms
NexaCore's standard vendor payment cycle is Net-45 days from receipt of a valid GST-compliant invoice and signed Goods/Service Receipt Note (GRN). Advance payments exceeding 20% of contract value require CFO exception approval.
