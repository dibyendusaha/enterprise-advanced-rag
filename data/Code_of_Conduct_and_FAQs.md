# Code of Conduct, Ethics, Compliance & HR FAQs

## 1. Conflict of Interest & Moonlighting Prohibition
Full-time employees must dedicate their primary professional efforts to NexaCore. Secondary employment, paid consulting, advisory board seats, or freelance software development for external commercial entities is strictly prohibited without prior written disclosure and approval from Legal and HR.

## 2. Gift, Hospitality & Anti-Bribery Thresholds
Under NexaCore's Anti-Bribery and Anti-Corruption (ABAC) policy, employees may accept nominal promotional gifts from vendors valued below INR 2,500 (or USD 30) once per year. Cash gifts, gift cards, loans, or lavish hospitality of any value are strictly prohibited and must be declined and reported to Compliance.

## 3. Whistleblower & POSH Redressal Channels
NexaCore maintains a zero-tolerance policy toward workplace harassment and ethical violations. Complaints under the Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH) Act can be filed confidentially at posh@nexacore.internal, and anonymous ethics/whistleblower reports can be submitted 24x7 via ethicsline.nexacore.internal without fear of retaliation.

## 4. Social Media & External Speaking Policy
Employees speaking at technical conferences, publishing open-source libraries, or posting about NexaCore on LinkedIn/X must include a personal-views disclaimer and must never disclose non-public architecture diagrams, client names, or internal metrics without Corporate Communications approval.

## 5. FAQ: Notice Period, Buyout & Exit Formalities
The standard notice period for confirmed employees is 60 days (30 days during probation). Notice period buyout or early release is discretionary and requires formal project handover sign-off from the Delivery Manager and Business Unit HR. Full and Final (FnF) settlement is processed within 30 days of the last working day.

## 6. FAQ: Payroll Cutoffs & IT Helpdesk Escalation
Monthly attendance regularization and investment/tax proof declarations close on the 20th of every month; salaries are credited on the last working day of the month. For laptop hardware failures, MFA resets, or VPN lockouts, raise a P1 ticket at helpdesk.nexacore.internal or call the 24x7 IT bridge at ext. 4400.
