# Code of Conduct, Ethics & HR FAQs

## Conflict of Interest & Moonlighting
Full-time employees must not engage in secondary employment, paid consulting, or freelance software development for external commercial entities without written disclosure and approval from Legal and HR.

## Gift & Hospitality Threshold
Employees may accept nominal promotional gifts from vendors valued below INR 2,500 (or USD 30). Cash gifts or gift cards of any value are strictly prohibited.

## FAQ: Notice Period & Exit Formalities
The standard notice period for confirmed employees is 60 days (30 days during probation). Buyout of notice period is subject to project handover sign-off by the Delivery Manager and Business Unit HR.

## FAQ: IT Helpdesk Escalation
For laptop hardware failures or VPN lockouts, raise a P1 ticket at helpdesk.nexacore.internal or call the 24x7 IT bridge at ext. 4400.
